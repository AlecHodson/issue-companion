      Critical severity indicators — know these

      If any of these phrases apply to your situation, the issue is likely Critical.

      Health & Safety
      Fire door failed, gas leak, flood, structural damage, child injured, ambulance, RIDDOR, evacuation, intruder, lock-in/out
      Safeguarding
      Child missing, disclosure, allegation against staff, police, social services, LADO, MASH
      Ratios
      Ratio breach, below ratio, uncovered room, no qualified staff in room
      Data Protection
      Personal data shared externally, lost device with data, misdirected email with personal data, ICO notification required

      If Copilot can't find a match

      When Copilot flags a content gap (no confident policy match), the issue is still logged and you'll be told to contact your Area Manager directly. These gaps are reviewed monthly to update the knowledge base — so your log still helps even if guidance is limited today.
